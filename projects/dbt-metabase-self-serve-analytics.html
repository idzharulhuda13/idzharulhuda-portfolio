Case study

DBT + Metabase Self-Serve Analytics

50+ reports moved to BigQuery, daily to hourly, with 40+ stakeholders self-serving.

Role
Analytics engineer, Rey.id
Stack
dbt, BigQuery, Metabase, SQL

Problem and constraints

15 analysts spent 2+ hours daily running manual reports from Google Sheets. Stakeholders couldn't access data until afternoon reports were ready.

Approach

Migrated 50+ reports to BigQuery with dbt transformations (staging, intermediate, marts). Built automated data quality checks. Deployed Metabase for self-serve exploration.

Architecture

Measured result

Reports run hourly instead of daily. 40+ stakeholders self-serve. 30% overall pipeline efficiency gain.

What Value Scope and source
reports migrated 50+ from manual Google Sheets workflows to scheduled BigQuery models with dbt transformations
stakeholders self-serving 40+ querying dbt models and Metabase dashboards without an analyst
analysts previously running manual reports 15 2+ hours a day each before the migration
report refresh cadence daily to hourly the same reports after moving off hand-assembled Google Sheets
